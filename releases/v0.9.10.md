# v0.9.10

The menu's colours are back, and they are the right ones.

## Fixed

- **The state dots vanished and the counts were pushed out of view.**
  A misplaced brace left the panel's variables — the state colours,
  the text colours, the hairlines, the monospace stack — defined
  inside a media block rather than at the root, so none of them
  applied. Introduced in v0.9.9.

## Changed

- **Dirty is the sheet purple**, not amber, which has not been the
  dirty colour anywhere in the bearo world since the purple took it.
- **Trouble is the rust** the terminal uses — deeper on a light menu,
  lifted on a dark one — in place of a flat red that sat heavy on
  both.

## Install

```sh
brew tap bearocratic/tap
brew install den
# or upgrade
brew upgrade den
```
